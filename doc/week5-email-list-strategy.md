
# Week 5 — FreshFit Email Marketing

## 1. Email-list strategy

### Objective
Build a mailing list of people interested in activewear and fitness.

### How FreshFit collects email addresses
- Add a newsletter sign-up form to the website.
- Ask visitors to enter their email address.
- Let subscribers choose their interests.
- Obtain permission before sending marketing emails.

### What subscribers receive
- New collection announcements.
- Activewear product information.
- Fitness and lifestyle inspiration.
- Promotional news and special offers.

### How success will be measured
- Number of new subscribers.
- Email delivery rate.
- Email open rate.
- Click-through rate.
- Website visits and purchases from campaigns.

## 2. Audience segments

### Segment 1: New subscribers
People who have recently joined the mailing list.

Content: Welcome email and introduction to FreshFit.

### Segment 2: Fitness enthusiasts
People interested in activewear and fitness essentials.

Content: Product highlights and fitness inspiration.

### Segment 3: Returning customers
People who have previously purchased from FreshFit.

Content: New collections and relevant offers.

### Segment 4: Interested visitors
Subscribers who have shown interest but have not purchased.

Content: Product information and relevant promotions.

## 5. Email campaign workflow

1. A visitor opens the FreshFit website.
2. The visitor submits the newsletter form and gives consent.
3. The email platform stores the subscriber and their interests.
4. The subscriber receives the welcome email.
5. Wait two days.
6. Send a relevant promotional email.
7. Measure deliveries, opens, clicks and purchases.
8. Use the results to improve the next campaign.

Implementation note: The website currently demonstrates
sign-up in the browser. Real email automation requires
an email marketing platform or backend integration.
