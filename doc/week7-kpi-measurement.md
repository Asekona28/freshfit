
# Week 7 — FreshFit Marketing Analytics

## 1. Campaign objective

Proposed objective:

During a two-week demonstration campaign, encourage
visitors to join the FreshFit newsletter and explore
the activewear collection.

This is a proposed objective, not a claim about actual
campaign performance.

## 2. KPI framework

### KPI 1: Email delivery rate
Formula:

Emails delivered / emails sent x 100

Purpose: Measures how many sent emails were delivered.

### KPI 2: Email open rate
Formula:

Unique opens / emails delivered x 100

Purpose: Helps assess audience interest in the email.
Privacy features can affect the accuracy of this metric.

### KPI 3: Click-through rate
Formula:

Unique clicks / emails delivered x 100

Purpose: Measures how often recipients click email links.

### KPI 4: Newsletter sign-up conversion rate
Formula:

New sign-ups / unique landing-page visitors x 100

Purpose: Measures how effectively the website attracts
newsletter subscribers.

### KPI 5: Purchase conversion rate
Formula:

Purchases attributed to the campaign / emails delivered x 100

Purpose: Estimates the campaign's contribution to purchases
when reliable tracking is available.

## 3. Sample dashboard data

These figures are fictional and are included for

Emails delivered: 950

Unique clicks: 76

Open rate:
380 / 950 x 100 = 40%

Click-through rate:
76 / 950 x 100 = 8%

Purchase conversion rate:
19 / 950 x 100 = 2%

## 4. Campaign measurement plan

1. Record the campaign date and target audience.
2. Record emails sent and delivered.
3. Record unique opens and clicks.
4. Use tagged links to distinguish campaign visits.
5. Record sign-ups and purchases when tracking is available.
6. Compare the results with the campaign objective.
7. Document limitations and make recommendations.

## 5. Initial recommendations

- Test different subject lines.
- Make the main email button easy to find.
- Link email buttons directly to relevant products.
- Compare engagement across audience segments.
- Replace sample data with measured campaign results
  when real results become available.

## 6. Data storage limitation

The current newsletter form stores demonstration
sign-ups in the browser's localStorage.

This data is not shared across devices and is not
a secure central database.

A backend or database integration is required for
persistent central customer records.
