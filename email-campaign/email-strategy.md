
# FreshFit Email Marketing Strategy

## 1. Campaign Objective
The goal is to build relationships with customers, introduce FreshFit products, and encourage potential customers to visit the website.

## 2. Target Audience
- People interested in fitness and activewear.
- Visitors who voluntarily subscribe to FreshFit emails.
- Existing customers who agree to receive marketing emails.

## 3. Audience Segmentation

### New Subscribers
Send a welcome email introducing FreshFit and its products.

### Interested Shoppers
Send product information and collection updates to subscribers who have shown interest in particular products, where this information is available.

### Existing Customers
Send relevant product updates and promotional emails to customers who have consented to receive them.

## 4. Email Campaign Types

### Welcome Email
Thank new subscribers, introduce the brand, and invite them to explore the website.

### Promotional Email
Highlight FreshFit products, prices, and a call to action to view the collection.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Delivery rate
- Email open rate
- Click-through rate
- Website visits from email
- Purchases attributed to email, if online purchasing is implemented

## 6. Measurement
Review campaign results after sending emails. Compare opens, clicks, website visits, and purchases to identify what works best.

## 7. Privacy
Only send marketing emails to people who have agreed to receive them. Include a clear unsubscribe option and protect subscriber information.

## 8. Expected Outcome
Increase awareness of FreshFit, encourage website visits, and build stronger customer relationships.
