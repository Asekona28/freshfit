
# FreshFit Email Campaign Workflow

## 1. Customer Signs Up
A customer visits the FreshFit website and enters their email address into the newsletter sign-up form.

## 2. Email Address Is Collected
The customer's email address is collected after they give permission to receive marketing emails.

## 3. Welcome Email Is Sent
The customer receives a welcome email introducing FreshFit and its products.

## 4. Customer Is Segmented
Subscribers are grouped into:
- New subscribers
- Interested shoppers
- Existing customers

## 5. Promotional Email Is Sent
Customers receive promotional emails about FreshFit products and special offers.

## 6. Customer Visits the Website
The email includes a button that directs customers to the FreshFit products page.

## 7. Campaign Performance Is Measured
FreshFit checks:
- Number of emails delivered.
- Number of emails opened.
- Number of people clicking links.
- Number of purchases made.

## 8. Improvements
FreshFit uses the results to improve future email campaigns.

# Testing Checklist

- [ ] Welcome email displays correctly.
- [ ] Promotional email displays correctly.
- [ ] Product names and prices are correct.
- [ ] Shop Now button opens the products page.
- [ ] Email layout works on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Newsletter form displays correctly.
- [ ] Unsubscribe option is included in real marketing emails.
